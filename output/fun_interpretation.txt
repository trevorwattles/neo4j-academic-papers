<b>What this shows:</b> the most common CS pairing is cs.DM (discrete math) + cs.DS (data structures &amp; algorithms) with 104 shared papers, and the top three pairs are all theory (DM / DS / CC). cs.AI + cs.LG is only 8th (52 papers). That fits the data: this sample covers arXiv papers from April 2007 to December 2008, years before deep learning made cs.LG the biggest category on arXiv. Bonus: cs.IT is the largest CS category (1,487 papers) because every cs.IT paper is also tagged math.IT, the alias for the same Information Theory archive.
